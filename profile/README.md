# Shopinator

Shopinator builds apps for the Shopify App Store. It is a brand of Christian Schwan in Freiburg, Germany.

## Apps

### Checkout Address Validator

Blocks checkout when a delivery address has no house number, even on Shop Pay, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
Rules are set per country, and buyer messages come in 33 languages. For Shopify merchants whose parcels come back
because an address had no house number.

[Checkout Address Validator on the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/checkout-address-validator-1)

### Delivery Promise

Coming soon.

Shows shoppers an estimated delivery date on the storefront, in checkout and after purchase. For Shopify merchants
who want shoppers to see when an order will arrive.
